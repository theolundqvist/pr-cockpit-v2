## Caveats / non-blocking observations

- **Tree-sitter grammars are not checked in.** `scripts/fetch-grammars.sh`
  fetches them lazily into `apps/desktop/src/assets/grammars/` but the
  workspace tarball ships without that directory. During my Playwright
  smoke, the page logged `[404] GET /assets/grammars/tree-sitter-rust.wasm`
  three times — i.e., the highlighter fell back silently. The diff
  renders and scrolls; the highlight pass is unverified end-to-end in
  *this* run. The committed M1 smoke artifacts under `artifacts/m1-smoke/`
  do show highlighted lines because that recording was produced with the
  grammars present. Not a budget failure, but worth flagging: CI must
  continue to invoke `scripts/fetch-grammars.sh` before perf/smoke jobs.
- **No real native Tauri window was booted.** This cloud-agent VM only
  has headless WebKit. The IPC contract, the renderer, the Svelte UI,
  the virtualized diff, the comrak pipeline, and the perf benchmarks are
  all exercised end-to-end against the same WebKit Tauri uses, but a
  human-eye pass against a real Tauri window on macOS/Linux desktop is
  still owed before shipping to users. (Matches the upstream verifier's
  same caveat.)
- **Diff fixture only contains `.rs` and `.ts` files.** The `.md`
  highlight path in the diff viewer is exercised by comrak (200-corpus
  regression) and by the markdown timeline render, but not by the diff
  viewer's in-line tree-sitter pipeline. Reasonable M1-scoped gap; the
  upstream handoff already flags it as a follow-up.

## Verdict

All 16 acceptance criteria from the M1 brief are met by observable
behavior on this fresh VM. The strongest claim my evidence supports is
`unit-test-verified` — every automated suite (rust unit + integration,
frontend unit, specta type generation + typecheck + svelte-check + lint,
markdown corpus regression, perf benchmark against PLAN §10) is green,
and the Playwright headless WebKit smoke flow works end-to-end. I do not
round up to `live-ui-verified` because (a) there is no real Tauri window
in this env and (b) the tree-sitter highlighter regressed silently
during my smoke due to missing grammar wasm.
